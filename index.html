How to use Load Tracker
1
No setup needed
Scanning runs through the app's own server — you don't need an API key. (Owner/advanced: a personal Anthropic key can be saved in the Upload tab as a backup.)
2
Drag in your settlement PDF
Drop your settlement PDF into the upload box. The AI reads all the loads, rates, tons, FSC, and totals automatically.
3
Drag in your ticket photos
Drop your load ticket photos (JPG, PNG) into the ticket box. The AI reads each one and matches it to the settlement automatically.
4
Check your dashboard
Your dashboard shows YTD earnings, total loads, tons, and any discrepancies between your tickets and what you were paid.
5
Repeat every week
Upload each new settlement and ticket photos as you get them. Everything builds up over time so you have a full year view.
💡 Your data saves automatically in this browser. Bookmark this page to come back anytime.
