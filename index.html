How to use this app

1
Paste the Amrize text. Copy the dispatch text exactly as Amrize sent it (either the short one-liner or the full structured message) and paste it into the box.
2
Tap Expand dispatch. The app looks up the pickup and delivery locations in your address book and fills in the full addresses automatically.
3
Check the fields. If anything shows a "check this" badge, it means the app couldn't confidently match that location — fix it by hand before sending. A green "matched" badge means it came straight from your address book.
4
Assign loads to drivers. Type how many loads each driver gets. The "Assigned X of Y loads" bar turns green once the numbers match your total dispatch quantity.
5
Save assignment to LoadTracker. This writes the assignment into a new "Dispatches" tab in your Sheet — it does not touch your Tickets, Drivers, or Settlements tabs.
6
Send the messages. Scroll to "Messages to send" — each driver gets their own copy-paste ready message with just their load count.
7
Check completion. Pick a date and tap "Check completion" to see how many of each driver's assigned loads actually show up as hauled in LoadTracker's Tickets tab.
✓
The green badge at the top of "Assign loads to drivers" means the driver list is live from your Sheet. Amber means it couldn't connect and is showing a saved copy instead.
